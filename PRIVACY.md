# Sonic Shielding Privacy Policy

Sonic Shielding processes audio from browser tabs that the user explicitly protects. Processing occurs locally and in real time on the user's device. Sonic Shielding does not record, retain, transmit, sell, or share audio.

Comfort-profile settings and the list of currently protected tabs are stored locally through Chrome extension storage. Sonic Shielding does not collect browsing history, analytics, personal information, or health information, and does not use a remote server.

Removing the extension removes its local extension data subject to the browser's storage behavior. Questions may be submitted through the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing.

Last updated: August 17, 2026.
