How Sonic Shielding works

Primary protection

Beep blocker

Sonic Shielding analyzes protected tab audio locally. When it finds a prominent beep or narrow harsh tone, it briefly reduces only the detected frequencies. A short lookahead helps catch the beginning, and speech preservation reduces unnecessary filtering of voices.

Protection strengthLow, Balanced, and Strong provide quick combinations of sensitivity and maximum tone reduction.
Tone detection sensitivityHigher values react to less-prominent tones. Lower values require clearer evidence of a beep.
Maximum tone reductionThe greatest percentage by which a detected tone may be reduced. It does not continuously reduce all audio.
Lowest protected frequencyTones below this frequency are ignored. Detection continues up to 16 kHz so very high electronic alarms are covered.
Release durationHow long suppression fades out after a tone is no longer detected. Longer releases sound smoother; shorter releases recover faster.
Sudden sound reductionReduces unusually strong broadband peaks by the selected percentage. This works separately from narrow-tone detection.

Preserve speech is enabled by default. Leave it on unless you need more aggressive detection around voices.

Per tab

Turn protection on

Open the pinned shield while viewing a tab and select Protect this tab. Enable it separately on every tab you want protected. Red means off; blue means active. Protection stays active on that tab while you visit other tabs.

Optional

Permanent comfort EQ

The lower profile section can continuously reduce selected frequency ranges. Its suggested settings heavily soften upper frequencies to cover many piercing, high-pitched sounds. It starts off because permanent filtering can also muffle speech and music.

Settings

Autosave and live updates

Autosave is enabled by default. Changes are stored locally and immediately sent to every actively protected tab. Turn autosave off if you prefer to apply a group of changes with the Save button.

Efficiency & privacy

Local, idle-aware processing

Audio is processed on your device and is never recorded or uploaded. When a protected tab stops playing sound, Sonic Shielding pauses audio processing and releases unused resources, then restores them when playback resumes.

Works in Brave and Chrome. Browser-internal and extension-store pages cannot be captured. Sonic Shielding is a comfort tool, not a medical device or calibrated loudness meter.
