Sonic Shielding - Comfort profile

Softer sound where you need it.
Use care with sound. This is a comfort tool, not a medical or hearing test. Begin low and stop immediately if uncomfortable.

Protection

Sonic Shielding now listens for prominent beeps and harsh tones, then briefly softens only the detected sound. Normal speech and music pass through unchanged whenever possible.

Advanced settings

Optional EQ reductions remain active continuously. They are off by default because strong permanent cuts can muffle speech.
