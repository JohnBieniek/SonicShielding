Sonic Shielding - Comfort profile

Softer sound where you need it.
Use care with sound. This is a comfort tool, not a medical or hearing test. Begin low and stop immediately if uncomfortable.

Beep blocker

Two protections handle different kinds of uncomfortable sound.

Tone-specific protection

For beeps, electronic dings, and squeals. It finds the offending frequencies and briefly reduces only those tones—not the entire sound.

Widens suppression and briefly reduces the whole sound when several alarm-like tones are detected. This can make other audio sound quieter while an alarm is present.

Whole-sound spike protection

For abrupt clicks, impacts, and loud bursts. It briefly reduces the whole sound when the overall signal spikes—not just one frequency.

Optional comfort EQ

These reductions stay active continuously when enabled. The suggested settings heavily soften upper frequencies to cover many piercing, high-pitched sounds. The EQ starts off because permanent filtering can also muffle speech and music.
